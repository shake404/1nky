# Boards, beef & happenings

Flicks are one half of the place. Talking is the other.

## Boards

**Boards** is the list of walls. Cities get their own cards with counts on them;
everything else sits under **Everything else** as chips.

A board isn't something you apply for. **You start one by posting to it** — put a
flick up with a city on it, or start a thread on a slug that doesn't exist yet, and
the board exists.

Each board has two sides:

- **talk** — threads and replies.
- **flicks** — what got put up with that board's tag on it.

## Threads

**Start one** on any board. Title, body, up. Replies nest under each other, and a
reply can quote and answer a specific one rather than the thread as a whole.

Reply counts show on the board list, so you can see where the noise is without
opening anything.

When you **reply** — in a thread, or in the comments under a flick — you can
[shout somebody out](/guide/talking) by typing `@`, and the app suggests the writers
already in that conversation.

## Beef

Beef doesn't stick around. When you start a thread you pick how long it runs:

| Choice | What it says |
|---|---|
| **24 hours** | gone tomorrow |
| **3 days** | a weekend of it |
| **a week** | settle it properly |
| **pinned** | stays up |

A thread with a clock on it shows a countdown — "dies in 23h" — and when the clock
runs out **the wall throws it away itself.** It's not hidden from you while quietly
sitting in a database; the box that holds it drops it, and the index drops it after.
Nothing to restore, nothing to subpoena, no cron job anybody forgot to run.

Boards where things are meant to stay put open on **pinned** instead. Beef is the
default where beef is the point.

::: tip Buffing vs. running out
Buffing is you taking something down. A clock running out is the thread taking
itself down. Neither one can reach a screenshot somebody else already took.
:::

## Happenings

**Happenings** is jams, meets and shows with a date on them. Soonest first,
filterable by city.

A happening isn't a different kind of post — it's a thread with a date. Start a
thread on the board you want it on, flip the date switch, put the date on it. It
lands on the happenings list, and it **clears itself a week after** the date
passes. Nobody has to sweep up last month's flyers.

## Holler

**Holler at us** is the feedback board, sitting inside the app on the same rails as
everything else. Your tag, your words, done — no email, no account, no separate
identity of any kind.

It's the right channel for "this is broken on my phone," "this word is confusing,"
"the feed does something weird after thirty posts," and feature requests. Requests
get triaged onto the [roadmap](/roadmap), which is the actual backlog.

It's the wrong channel for anything security-sensitive. That goes through
[responsible disclosure](/security) instead, privately, because a published
deanonymization bug is a danger to people who can't patch themselves.

You can buff a holler later if you change your mind, same as any other post.
