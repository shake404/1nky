# Crews

A crew is a tag that more than one writer holds. It posts, it has a page, it has a
roster, and it works the same way your own tag does — made on a device, held on
devices, never on our side.

## Starting one

**Crew → Start a crew.** Pick a name, write a line or two, choose a passphrase, and
it's up.

What comes back is the crew's **blackbook** — the same kind of file as your own,
for the crew's tag instead of yours. It downloads immediately and it's also shown
as a scannable block you can print or photograph.

::: danger This is the whole crew
Lose the crew blackbook and the crew is gone. Nobody can recover it. Hand it to
somebody who shouldn't have it and they *are* the crew. Treat it accordingly.
:::

Crew names aren't unique either. The crew's **mark** is what tells one FASE from
another.

## Handing it out

Anybody who holds the crew's blackbook can post as the crew and run it. To put
somebody in, hand them the blackbook — however you'd hand a writer anything —
and they bring it in from **Crew → Bring in a crew blackbook**.

There's a link to that on any crew page you don't already hold: *"Hold this crew's
blackbook? Bring it in."*

## Who runs it

**Whoever holds the book.** There is no owner account, no admin flag, no founder
privilege that outlasts the file.

That's deliberate: an ownership row on our side would be a thing to seize, a thing
to argue about, and a thing we'd have to keep. Instead, co-management works exactly
the way it works in real life — you gave somebody the book, so now they run it too.
Same roster, same say.

You take it back by buffing them off the roster. You can't take the file back, so
be sure before you hand it over.

## Repping vs. the roster {#repping-vs-the-roster}

Two different claims, deliberately never merged.

| | Who says it | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Reppin'** | The writer says they're down with the crew | Their wall, and the crew page under "Reppin'" |
| **Roster** | The crew says so, signed by the crew's tag | The crew page under "Roster" |

Anyone can **Rep this crew** on any crew page, and it turns up on their own wall as
a claim. That's it — a claim. Merging the two lists would quietly upgrade every
unilateral claim into the crew's endorsement, which is exactly the thing a roster is
supposed to mean.

**Putting someone on the roster** (the crew's say) happens on the crew page when
you hold the book: paste the writer's link or tag id, hit **Look up**, check their
mark matches the one on their wall, then **Put someone on**. A writer already
repping can be lifted onto the roster in one tap.

Check the mark, not the name. That's the whole point of it.

## Posting as a crew

The **speaking as** chip lives in the top bar. Tap it and you get a menu: **You**,
plus every crew whose blackbook this device is holding. Pick a crew and the chip
turns into **posting as FASE**, with a **back to you** button next to it.

While you're switched over, flicks, clips, threads and replies go up under the
crew's tag instead of yours.

Things worth knowing:

- **Your own tag is never touched.** The switch points at a crew's book for the
  session; it doesn't replace what your device holds. Reload and you're back on
  your own tag.
- **Messages always come from your own tag.** Private word is between people, and
  a shared inbox for a shared name is a bad idea in a place like this. So the
  switcher deliberately doesn't apply to [messages](/guide/talking#messages).
- **You can buff a crew post** as long as you still hold that crew's book.
- If a crew's book gets removed from the device while it's the active one, the app
  falls back to your own tag and tells you so.

## Crew pages

Every crew has a page: the crew's picture, name, mark, bio, when it was founded,
the roster, who's reppin', and everything the crew put up.

Crew pictures go through the same **cropper** and the same on-device scrub as your
own avatar — drag, zoom, up. If you hold the book, you can change the crew's name,
bio and picture from the crew page.

## Across your devices

Holding a crew on your phone used to mean not holding it on your laptop. Now it
follows you: the crew's book is locked to **your own tag** and left on the wall,
and any device you've restored your tag on pulls it down and unlocks it on its own.

- You don't do anything. It happens when you open the app.
- Only your tag can unlock it. Ours can't, and nobody else's can.
- Crews founded before this existed get seeded automatically the next time you
  open the app on the device that holds them.

You can still bring a crew in by hand from a blackbook file at any time — that's
how somebody *else* gets put in, and it's the fallback if a device is being stubborn.
