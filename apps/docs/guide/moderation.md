# Keeping it clean

Anonymous doesn't mean unmoderated. Here's every lever, who pulls it, and what it
actually does.

## Flag it

**Flag it** sits on a flick or clip's own page and on a thread. Open the thing, one
tap, then a reason:

Straight-up illegal · Nudity · Spam · Biting / impersonation · Sketchy file ·
Slurs · Something else

You can add one line of detail. Then it's in the queue, and *"Flagged. Someone will
look at it."*

Flags are a post like any other — signed by your tag, carrying no address, no
device, nothing about where you were when you sent it. Flagging somebody doesn't
tell them you did.

## Ignore this writer

**Ignore this writer** is the personal one — on a writer's own wall, or on a flick
they put up. Their stuff stops appearing for you. It follows your tag rather than the
browser you're in, so it holds on your other devices too.

It's reversible: **Stop ignoring**, and they're back. Everybody you've ignored is
listed under **Setup → Ignored writers**.

This is a filter on your own wall, not a report. Nobody gets told, nothing goes in a
queue, and if you want somebody actually dealt with, flag them as well.

## Buff this

**Buff this** is you taking your own post down. It comes off the wall and out of
the index, and it doesn't come back.

Two limits worth being straight about:

- It can't reach a copy somebody else already saved. Screenshots are forever
  everywhere, including here.
- A mod can buff your post too, if it's the kind of thing that gets buffed.

## What mods can do

Flags land in a queue that a small number of mods work through. They can take a
post down and they can ban the tag that put it up. A banned tag gets refused at the
door on the next thing it tries to put up — not hidden, refused.

Mods see the flagged post, the reason, and how long the flagging tag has been
around. They don't see an address, a location or a device, because none of that
exists to be shown. The moderation screen only opens on a device that's already been
set up to moderate — there's no login form to attack, and no list of moderators to
lean on.

**Zero tolerance, no discretion, no appeal:** any sexual content involving minors.
It's removed, the tag is banned, and it's reported to the NCMEC CyberTipline. Full
detail on the [terms page](/legal/terms).

## Getting put on

**Setup → Put someone on** mints a one-writer put-on: a code and a link. Hand it to
one writer.

When somebody starts a tag through a put-on, they **skip the line** — the extra wait
a brand-new tag normally does before it's trusted goes away. On the pick-a-tag
screen it just says *"Somebody put you on. You skip the line on your first post."*

Why it exists:

- **It's anti-spam.** A spam farm can't be vouched for, and vouching leaves a trail
  between the voucher and the vouched-for — so a whole spam tree can be dropped in
  one action rather than one tag at a time.
- **It's how this kind of place is meant to fill up.** Somebody puts you on. That's
  the tradition; it's not a growth hack.

Put-ons are good for one writer each. Old ones stay listed on your Setup screen so
you can tell who you've already handed one to.

You do **not** need a put-on to use 1NKY. Anybody can pick a tag and post; a put-on
just skips the newcomer wait.

## What we can't do

Worth saying in a moderation page, because it cuts both ways:

- We can remove content from 1NKY. We can't take back a screenshot somebody saved.
- We can ban a tag. We can't ban a person, because we don't know any people — a new
  tag is a new tag. That's the honest cost of no accounts, and the reason bans lean
  on the wait-and-vouch system instead of on identity.
- We can't tell you who somebody is, and neither can anybody who asks us.
  [That's the whole architecture.](/privacy/no-logs#subpoena-posture)
