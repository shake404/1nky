# Shout-outs & messages

Two different things. One is public and one nobody can read but you.

## Shout-outs

Type `@` in a comment or a reply and a short list of the writers already in that
thread drops down. Pick one and their tag lands in what you're writing — and
because you picked a real writer instead of just typing letters, the post that goes
up carries an actual reference to them.

They find out. Not by email, because there isn't one; by a **speech-bubble glyph in
the top bar with a dot on it** when somebody's said their name.

**Shout-outs** is that screen. Your name coming up, newest first.

- **Plain replies to your own posts don't land here.** Those are already sitting
  under the thing you put up. Only deliberate mentions do.
- The dot clears when you open the screen. The "last looked" stamp lives on your
  device, not on our side.
- Nothing about this involves a notification service, a push provider, or a
  third-party anything. There's nowhere to send a push to and nobody to send it.

## Messages {#messages}

**Word** is private messages. End to end, and the "end to end" is the load-bearing
part.

What our box actually holds for a message is a sealed envelope signed by a
throwaway one-shot name, addressed to the recipient and nothing else, with a
timestamp deliberately smeared by up to two days. So the honest description of what
the server knows is: *somebody sent something to this tag, at some point in the last
48 hours.* Not who. Not when. Not what.

- **We cannot read your messages.** Not a policy — the contents are encrypted
  between the two devices and we're never handed a key.
- **We cannot tell who's talking to whom.** The sender's own tag isn't on the
  outside of the envelope.
- Messages aren't copied into the searchable index at all. They sit on the box and
  your app fetches its own.

::: warning What encryption doesn't fix
Whoever you're messaging can screenshot it, and a message is only as private as the
least careful device in the conversation. Encryption protects the wire, not the
person on the other end of it.
:::

### Messages always come from your own tag

If you're [posting as a crew](/guide/crews#posting-as-a-crew), private word still
goes out under **your** tag, on purpose. A shared inbox for a shared name means
every crew member reads everyone's private conversations, which is a worse idea here
than almost anywhere else.

So the top-bar switcher applies to what you put up publicly, never to word.
